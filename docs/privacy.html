Privacy-First & Offline-First

Privacy Policy — Wanderer's Ledger

Package ID: com.wanderledger.app  •  Effective Date: November 24, 2026  •  Contact: vg.life.apps@gmail.com

TL;DR: Wanderer's Ledger is an offline-first, ad-free personal travel journal. All of your trips, boarding passes, flight PDFs, hotel confirmations, expenses, receipts, and travel memories stay strictly on your device in local SQLite storage. We do not sell your data, run third-party ads, or track you across apps.

1. Information Stored On Your Device

Everything you create in Wanderer's Ledger is stored locally inside your phone's private application storage:

  • Travel Journal Records: Trip names, destinations, dates, flight itineraries, train/bus/ferry/car legs, hotel stays, city stops, loyalty program balances, and journal notes.
  • Private Expenses & Receipts: Multi-currency expense entries, budgets, and compressed receipt images. Spend data and receipts are strictly excluded from shared postcards and Instagram Studio captions.
  • Travel Photos: Polaroid memory photos you choose to import or capture are compressed locally on your device.
  • Preferences: Optional display name, home currency, and distance unit (km/mi).

2. On-Device Document & Barcode Scanning (100% Offline)

When you scan a boarding pass barcode, paper receipt, screenshot, or airline/hotel PDF using Smart Magic Scan, text recognition (Google ML Kit on-device OCR) and PDF page rendering run entirely on your device. Your PDFs, tickets, and receipts are never uploaded to any external OCR server.

3. Device Permissions & Why We Request Them

  • Camera: Used solely when you tap Smart Scan to read boarding pass barcodes, snap receipts, or capture travel memory photos.
  • Photos / Media Library: Used solely when you pick travel memories or booking screenshots to import, or when you save exported 1080p Instagram postcards/collages to your gallery.
  • Approximate / Precise Location (Optional): Used only when you choose to tag a travel memory with your current city. We never track your location in the background.
  • Internet (Optional): Used only for lightweight daily currency exchange rate updates (public Frankfurter/ECB rates, sending zero personal data) and optional cloud backup if you explicitly sign in later.

4. Third-Party Ads, Analytics & Data Sharing

  • Zero Ads: Wanderer's Ledger contains no third-party advertising SDKs and does not use your Android Advertising ID.
  • Zero Data Selling: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or travel history.
  • User-Initiated Sharing Only: Content leaves your device only when you explicitly tap Share (e.g., exporting a postcard, CSV spend report, or local JSON backup via your device's native share sheet).

5. Data Deletion & Control

Because your data lives directly on your device, you have complete, instant control at all times:

  • Delete Individual Items: Delete any trip, flight, booking, expense, or memory photo inside the app at any time (associated local image files are permanently deleted from storage).
  • Delete All Local Data: Open the Passport tab and tap Reset Data to permanently erase your entire local database and stored media immediately.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling Wanderer's Ledger removes all private app-stored data from your device.
  • If optional cloud sync is enabled in a future release and you wish to request deletion of cloud-synced records, email vg.life.apps@gmail.com with your account email and we will delete all cloud records promptly.

6. Children's Privacy

Wanderer's Ledger is intended for general travel journaling by users aged 13 and older (18+ recommended for personal expense and booking management) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we introduce optional cloud sync features or update this Privacy Policy, we will update the Effective Date above and publish the revised policy at this URL.
