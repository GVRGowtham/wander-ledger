Privacy-First & Offline-First

Privacy Policy — Wanderer's Ledger

Package ID: com.wanderledger.app  •  Effective Date: November 26, 2026  •  Contact: vg.life.apps@gmail.com

TL;DR: Wanderer's Ledger is an offline-first personal travel journal. All of your trips, boarding passes, flight PDFs, hotel confirmations, expenses, receipts, and travel memories stay strictly on your device in local SQLite storage — with optional personal backup directly to your own hidden Google Drive app folder. We do not sell your personal data or store your travelogue on our own servers.

1. Information Stored On Your Device

Everything you create in Wanderer's Ledger is stored locally inside your phone's private application storage:

  • Travel Journal Records: Trip names, destinations, dates, flight itineraries, train/bus/ferry/car legs, hotel stays, city stops, loyalty program balances, and journal notes.
  • Private Expenses & Receipts: Multi-currency expense entries, budgets, historical exchange-rate snapshots, and compressed receipt images. Spend data and receipts are strictly excluded from Studio postcards, Keepsake Magazine PDFs, and Instagram Studio exports.
  • Travel Photos: Polaroid memory photos you choose to import or capture are compressed locally on your device.
  • Preferences: Optional display name, appearance theme (Light / Dark / System), home currency, distance unit (km/mi), reminder settings, and optional Google Drive backup preferences.

2. On-Device Document & Barcode Scanning (100% Offline)

When you scan a boarding pass barcode, paper receipt, screenshot, or airline/hotel PDF using Smart Magic Scan, text recognition (Google ML Kit on-device OCR) and PDF page rendering run entirely on your device. Your PDFs, tickets, and receipts are never uploaded to any external OCR server.

3. Optional Google Sign-In & Personal Google Drive Backup

Signing in with Google is 100% optional — you can use every journaling, scanning, spend-tracking, map, and Studio feature completely offline without an account. If you choose to sign in with Google:

  • Minimal Account Info: We read only your basic Google profile (first name and email address) to display your connected account in Passport and detect existing personal backups when you reinstall or switch phones.
  • Restricted Hidden App Folder (drive.appdata): Backups (your travelogue database snapshot plus incremental compressed memory and receipt photos) are stored strictly inside Google Drive's private hidden application folder. Wanderer's Ledger cannot see, list, read, or modify any other files or folders in your Google Drive, and our developer never has access to your Drive data.
  • Automatic Daily Backup & Wi-Fi Control: If you opt in to Automatic Daily Backup, Android schedules quiet background backups directly from your phone to your Google Drive. When Upload full photos on Wi-Fi only is enabled (default), lightweight trip and expense data can sync on cellular while photo uploads wait for Wi-Fi.
  • Google API Limited Use Compliance: Wanderer's Ledger's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We never use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or AI model training.

4. Device Permissions

  • Camera: Used solely when you tap Smart Scan to read boarding pass barcodes, snap receipts, or capture travel memory photos.
  • Photos / Media Library: Used solely when you pick travel memories or booking screenshots to import, or when you save exported 1080p Instagram postcards/collages to your gallery.
  • Approximate / Precise Location (Optional): Used only when you choose to tag a travel memory with your current city. We never track your location in the background.
  • Notifications (Optional): Used solely for on-device offline travel reminders (24-hour flight check-in, 3-hour departure, and hotel check-in mornings).
  • Internet: Used for lightweight daily currency exchange rate updates (public Frankfurter/ECB rates, sending zero personal data), optional OpenStreetMap address lookups you explicitly trigger, and optional Google Drive backup/restore when signed in.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

  • No Data Selling: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or travel history.
  • Private Travel Journal: Your trips, receipts, expenses, and photos remain strictly on your device (and optionally in your own private Google Drive app folder).
  • User-Initiated Sharing Only: Content leaves your device only when you explicitly tap Share (e.g., exporting a Studio postcard, Keepsake Magazine PDF, or CSV spend report) or explicitly enable/trigger your personal Google Drive backup.

6. Data Deletion & Full Control

Because your data lives directly on your device (and optionally in your own personal Google Drive app folder), you have complete, instant control at all times:

  • Delete Individual Items: Delete any trip, flight, booking, expense, or memory photo inside the app at any time (associated local image files are permanently deleted from storage).
  • Delete Google Drive Backup: Open the Passport tab and tap Delete Cloud Backup to permanently erase your Wanderer's Ledger backup and backed-up photos from Google Drive immediately without touching data on your phone. You may also revoke app access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete All Local Data: Open the Passport tab and tap Reset Data to permanently erase your entire local database and stored media immediately.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling Wanderer's Ledger removes all private app-stored data from your device.
  • If you have any questions about data privacy or deletion, contact vg.life.apps@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Wanderer's Ledger is intended for general travel journaling by users aged 13 and older (18+ recommended for personal expense and booking management) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

When we add new optional features or update this Privacy Policy, we will update the Effective Date above and publish the revised policy at this URL.
